# D04 — First version: GEDCOM import, direct-ancestors view and journey view

- Date, owner: 2026-11-11, Paweł Ciepliński (founder, product owner)
- Context and the options weighed: the agent proposed three scopes for the first version: (1) GEDCOM import, a view of direct ancestors without their siblings, and a journey view showing where direct ancestors lived on a map and in time order; (2) smaller: GEDCOM import and the direct-ancestors view only, with the map and timeline next; (3) larger: option 1 plus family statistics. The founder listed the map and the timeline as separate features; the agent merged them into one journey view.
- Decision and why: the founder chose option 1, the agent's recommendation. The agent's basis: the direct-ancestors view answers the problem the founder described; the journey view answers the founder's stated question about where the family comes from; statistics had no described situation yet. The founder gave no further reason.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: family statistics (D03), documents and photos, the heatmap and the AI assistant come later. Revisit if the GEDCOM export lacks the places and dates the journey view needs (A02), or if MyHeritage already offers an ancestors-only view (A04).

# D01 — Antenat is for the founder's own family and for other people researching theirs

- Date, owner: 2026-11-11, Paweł Ciepliński (founder, product owner)
- Context and the options weighed: asked whether Antenat is a tool for the founder's own family or a product other people also use. The founder chose both.
- Decision and why: "Zarówno dla mojej rodziny, jak i dla innych, żeby mogli z tego korzystać do włąsnych celów." (quote; translation: Both for my family and for others, so they can use it for their own purposes.) No further reason was given.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: other users' needs are not yet researched; the founder's own experience supports the problem only for the founder. Revisit if conversations with other family researchers do not show the same needs. When to serve other users first is a separate, open scope question.

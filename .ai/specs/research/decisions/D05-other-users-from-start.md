# D05 — The first version serves other people from the start, with user accounts

- Date, owner: 2026-11-11, Paweł Ciepliński (founder, product owner)
- Context and the options weighed: the agent recommended a first version for the founder alone, with other users later, because other people's needs are untested and their uploaded trees raise open questions about living relatives' personal data. The alternative was accounts for other people from the first version.
- Decision and why: the founder chose to serve other people from the first version. No reason was given.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: how living relatives' personal data in uploaded trees is handled must be decided before the first version is planned (Q04 in the brief). The decision accepts building before other users' needs are researched; that acceptance is not evidence of those needs. Revisit when interviews with other family researchers have a result.

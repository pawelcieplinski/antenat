# Interview — founder (family history researcher, MyHeritage user), 2026-11-11

Limits: a single account. The person is both the user describing their own experience and the decision-maker for the product. Answers were written in a chat during the `om-discover` session, not given in a live interview; follow-up probes were limited to three rounds. Quotes are verbatim in Polish; the English after each quote is the interviewer's translation, not a quote.

- Recent situation or task the person described: researching their own family history. They keep their family tree in MyHeritage and look for records in online archives: szukajwarchiwach.gov.pl, genealogiawarchiwach.pl, FamilySearch and Geneteka.
  - Quote: "Używałem MyHeritage. Szukałem danych w archiwach (szukajwarchiwach.gov.pl, genealogiawarchiwach.pl oraz FamilySearch) i genetece." (Translation: I used MyHeritage. I searched for data in archives (…) and Geneteka.)
- What they did, step by step: not described beyond the tools above.
- What worked, what did not, and any consequences they described:
  - In MyHeritage they want to filter the tree, specifically to limit it to direct ancestors without the ancestors' siblings; they find browsing without that inconvenient.
  - Quote: "Brakuje mi w MyHeritage filtrowania drzewa oraz tych dodatkowych funkcji, o których wspomniałem." (Translation: In MyHeritage I miss tree filtering and the additional features I mentioned.)
  - Quote, answering what they last wanted to filter for: "Ograniczyć się tylko do przodków bez ich rodzeństwa. Jest to niewygodne do przeglądania." (Translation: To limit it to ancestors only, without their siblings. It is inconvenient to browse.)
  - No frequency, time lost or other cost was described.
- Other approaches they tried and how those worked: none described.
- The outcome they were trying to achieve and what happened:
  - The question they want a map, heatmap, timeline or AI assistant to answer: where the family comes from and how it ended up where it lives now. How they answer this question today was not described.
  - Quote: "Skąd pochodzi rodzina i jak znalazła się w miejscu, w którym jest teraz." (Translation: Where the family comes from and how it ended up in the place where it is now.)
- Verbatim quotes worth keeping (mark each as quote):
  - Quote, the idea in their words: "Chcę stworzyć aplikacje do wygodnego śledzenia losów swojej rodziny za pomocą drzewa genealogicznego, zebranych dokumentów i zdjęć, mapa z wędrówką rodziny oraz heatmapa przodków i oś czasu z miejscami, w których żyli przodkowie. W miarę możliwości, chciałbym dodać darmowego agenta ai, który byłby w stanie odczytywać akta oraz podpowiadać i odpowiadać na pytania dotyczące rodziny i ch historii."
  - Quote: "Antenat musi mieć też statystyki rodziny jak w MyHeritage" (Translation: Antenat must also have family statistics like in MyHeritage.)
- What they explicitly did not care about: not discussed.

  - The account covers the founder's own use. Whether other family researchers share these needs was not discussed and is not supported by this note.
  - Which MyHeritage statistics the founder uses, and how large their tree is, were not answered.

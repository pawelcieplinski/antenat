# Interview — founder (MyHeritage user), 2026-11-12: MyHeritage ancestor-view check

Limits: the founder's own report of checking MyHeritage's views on their tree (A04 in the product brief), written in the chat. No link, screenshot or list of the views checked was recorded, so this is the founder's account, not a checked benchmark. Quote verbatim in Polish; the English is the interviewer's translation, not a quote.

- Recent situation or task the person described: checked whether MyHeritage offers a view of direct ancestors without their siblings.
- What worked, what did not, and any consequences they described:
  - Quote: "w ograniczonym trybie do kuzynostwa, a ja chcę też ukryć rodzeństwo i innych małżonków" (Translation: in a limited mode, down to cousins, and I also want to hide siblings and other spouses.)
  - MyHeritage has a limited view that still includes relatives down to cousins. The founder wants a view that also hides siblings and other spouses.
- Interviewer's own remarks (kept separate from what was said):
  - "Other spouses" is read as spouses of an ancestor who are not themselves direct ancestors (for example a second husband or wife). The founder confirmed this reading on 2026-11-12.
  - What the limited mode is called, and exactly which relatives it shows, was not described.
